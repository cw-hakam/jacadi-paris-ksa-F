# Lille & Nest Storefront Features

Complete list of features available in the **Lille & Nest** Scandinavian organic innerwear storefront.

---

## 🛍️ E-Commerce Features

### Product Catalog & Innerwear Showcase
- **Organic Innerwear & Sleepwear Catalog** — Filterable by category (Organic Innerwear, Sleepwear & Pajamas, Toys & Games, Storybooks).
- **GOTS & Safety Badging** — GOTS 100% Organic Cotton, OEKO-TEX® Standard 100, and anti-chafe seam badges.
- **Product Details & High-Res Lookbooks** — Detailed product pages with Stitch lookbook photography zoom galleries.
- **Size & Fit Picker** — Size selector tailored for infants, toddlers, and young kids (0-2y, 2-4y, 4-8y, 8-12y).
- **Product Search & Filtering** — Real-time search across products, age categories, and badges.

### Shopping Experience
- **Shopping Cart Drawer (`CartContext`)** — Add, remove, and adjust item quantities in real time.
- **Wishlist Management** — Save favorite loungewear items across browsing sessions.
- **Encrypted Checkout Interface** — Clean checkout experience supporting promo codes and order summaries.

---

## 🎨 Design System & AI Design Integration

- **Nordic Slumber & Nest Palette** — Soft Terracotta (`#B86751`), Muted Sage (`#9DA893`), Canvas Cream (`#FBF9F5`), and Soft Linen (`#F5F1EA`).
- **Typography** — `Newsreader` (Editorial Serif Display) & `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body).
- **Google Stitch MCP Integration** — Direct workspace integration with Google Stitch MCP Server (`.agents/mcp_config.json`).
- **Dark & Light Mode** — Fully accessible dark theme tokens (`next-themes`).

---

## 📱 Responsive & Performance

- **Mobile-First Scandinavian Editorial Layout** — Fluid 12-column grid.
- **Production Optimized** — Single Page Application rewrites and cache headers configured in [`vercel.json`](../vercel.json).
- **Fast Build Times** — Built with Vite 5 and TypeScript 5.
